# DECISIONS.md — Assignment 4

Source of record for every judgement that shapes a graded artifact. An item exists here
only after the student rules on it; the ruling is recorded verbatim, and the artifact
(`LEDGER.md` entry, `REPORT.md` answer, `agent/*.py` choice, `prompts/` file) is derived
from this file, never the reverse.

Protocol (same shape as Assignment 2 and Assignment 3):

1. One item per turn. The assistant supplies the referenced content (the code comment,
   the trace line, the transcript quote), the options, and one recommendation.
2. The student answers with a ruling through the confirm/reject options, not free text.
3. The ruling is copied in verbatim under **Student's ruling**, with the artifact it
   produced. Nothing else in this file is authoritative.
4. `LEDGER.draft.md` (generated by `a4-ledger-watch`) supplies facts only; its `review`
   and `risk` fields stay `REVIEW_REQUIRED` until a ruling lands here.
5. Recorded runs are recorded once. A rejection and its reason are captured here at the
   moment they happen, because rubric B3 and REPORT §2 are checked against runA's trace.

Standing constraint from the course: AI tools are permitted and expected, and unreviewed
AI output or a fabricated ledger is an academic-integrity violation, so every entry below
names what was reviewed and what was rejected.
